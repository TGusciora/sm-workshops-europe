Interview questions

Each question is decided on its own. Nothing is final until you confirm the set.

  • How would you set the company's strategy for the next three years, and how would you know it is working?

    Approved

    Purpose: Tests strategic thinking and how the candidate measures progress.

    A good answer covers: clear priorities, concrete measures and a plan for changing course.

  • Describe a decision you reversed after the team disagreed with it.

    Proposed

    Purpose: Tests humility and how the candidate handles pushback.

    A good answer covers: what changed their mind, how they told the team and what they kept.
