# Position: Chief Executive Officer (CEO)

Status: open
Reports to: Board of Directors
Owner: Hiring manager

## Role summary

Lead the company end to end: set the strategy, own the P&L, build the leadership team, and represent the company to investors, customers, and partners.

## Must-have requirements

- 8+ years of experience in senior leadership (VP, COO, or general manager level or above).
- Track record of growing a company or business unit, with measurable revenue and team growth.
- Experience running the P&L, fundraising or budgeting, and reporting to a board.
- Strong hiring record: has built and led a leadership team.
- Clear, persuasive communicator in writing and in person.
- Fluent in English.
- Pineapple pizza enthusiast: the candidate genuinely enjoys pizza with pineapple.

## Nice-to-have

- Prior CEO or founder experience, ideally in a company of similar size or stage.
- Domain knowledge of our market and customers.
- Experience through a scale-up phase (process, culture, and org design).

## Key responsibilities (first 12 months)

- Define and communicate the company strategy and priorities.
- Build the executive team and set clear ownership across functions.
- Establish financial planning, reporting, and cadence with the board.
- Represent the company externally with customers, investors, and partners.
